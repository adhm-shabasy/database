A company stores information about its employees and departments.

Each employee is identified by a unique social security number. We also
store the employee's name (first name and last name), their salary, and
their home address, which consists of street, city, state, and ZIP code.
An employee can have several phone numbers.

Each department has a unique department ID and a name. A department can
be located in several different places.

Every employee works in exactly one department, but a department can
have many employees.
